Honest Assessment

When the Retainer Is Not the Right Choice

The retainer is $39 per month. For servers that do not need ongoing content, that is not efficient spending. Here is when you should look at something else instead.

  • 🔒 Your server's lore is fixed — you are not adding new locations, factions, or story events and do not plan to. A one-time purchase covers you.
  • 🎮 You only need one or two NPCs and are happy with them staying as-is once installed. An individual pack is the right call.
  • 👥 Your server is private, small, and non-commercial. The commercial rights and lore system are overhead you do not need to pay for.
  • 🚀 You need five characters at launch and then maintenance, not ongoing additions. The Server License is a better fit.
  • 💸 Your server is in early stage with uncertain revenue. Start with a free pack or an individual character, prove the concept, then upgrade.

FAQ

Retainer Questions

Everything you need to know before joining.

When does my first character arrive?

Within 24 hours of payment confirmation. Your first character and the world foundation document are delivered together via email and Discord. Subsequent characters arrive on a 30-day cycle from your start date.

Can I choose what character I get each month?

Yes. You can submit a brief — archetype, role, personality type, specific lore requirements — and we build toward it. You can also leave it open and we deliver the next character in the world sequence that makes the most narrative sense. Either approach works.

What does "unlimited revisions" actually mean?

No cap on the number of revision requests per month. No cap on which characters you can revise. Personality changes, new memories, config adjustments, lore sync updates, new edge case responses — all covered, no extra charge, turnaround within 24 hours.

How does monthly crypto payment work?

Each month we send you a payment request for $39. You pay in your chosen coin — the same 15 coins accepted for one-time purchases. On confirmation, your next month activates. No auto-billing — every payment is a conscious choice.

What plugins do the retainer characters support?

Currently SentiencePro and LlamaCraft Bridge, the same as all CharacterCraft products. As new plugins are added to the supported list, retainer members receive adapted configs for their entire library — automatically, no extra cost.

Do I keep the characters if I cancel?

Yes. Every character delivered during your active period is yours permanently — files, commercial rights, and all. What ends at cancellation is new monthly deliveries, revision access, and priority support. You keep everything already delivered.

Can I pause instead of cancelling?

We handle pauses on a case-by-case basis — reach out via Discord or email. For most situations we can defer a monthly delivery or hold your slot while your server is in a quiet period. Message us before the payment date and we will sort it.

What if a delivered character has issues after install?

Contact us. Retainer members get 8-hour response and we fix any issue with files we delivered — no charge. If it is broken and we built it, we repair it. Config issues, memory problems, unexpected behaviour — all covered under active retainer support.
