Can I choose any five characters from the roster?

Yes. You pick your five when you place the order. If fewer than five characters are currently available, your remaining slots are locked in and fulfilled as new characters launch — at no additional cost. You will never pay more than $24.99.

How long does delivery take?

Within 4 hours of blockchain confirmation. The full package — five character packs, world lore, relationship map, cost optimization report, and commercial license — arrives as a single download link via email and Discord.

What if my server is private or whitelisted?

A private or whitelisted server with no monetization technically falls under personal use. You could use individual packs instead. However, if you plan to monetize eventually, the license is worth securing now at the current price.

Does the license cover future characters I add?

The license covers the five characters in your pack. If you want to add more characters later — beyond your five slots — those are purchased separately at the standard individual price, with a commercial add-on available for license holders at a discount.

What plugins does the license support?

Currently SentiencePro and LlamaCraft Bridge. Every character in the license is delivered with configs optimized for both. As new plugins are supported, license holders receive adapted configs automatically at no cost.

What happens in the setup consultation?

A 30-minute Discord voice or text session with the character author. We walk through installation, config tuning for your specific server setup, memory retrieval settings, and any server-specific edge cases you want to address. Notes delivered within 24 hours.

Can I modify the character files?

Yes. The files are yours. Edit personalities, add or change memories, adjust configs — anything needed to fit your world. The only restriction is redistribution or resale of the files themselves.

What does "same-day support" mean in practice?

Server License holders get responses within business hours on the same calendar day for any issue — install problems, config questions, character behaviour that feels off. If we built it and it is broken, we fix it at no charge.
